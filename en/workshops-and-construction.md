<p class="lead">In workshops, construction, repairs, maintenance and small factories, the quote, the job and the invoice are about the same piece of work, but they tend to live in different places. We build simple, made-to-measure tools so the data is typed once.</p>

## Problems we solve

- Quotes made one by one, from an old quote that gets edited.
- Prices of materials and services scattered across several files.
- Hours and materials for each job recorded on paper or at the end of the day.
- The client's details typed in the quote, the invoice and the job record.

## What a tool can do

- **Quotes in minutes:** with a single price list and the calculations (margins, VAT, discounts) done by the company's own rules.
- **From quote to job:** an accepted quote becomes a job without retyping anything.
- **Hours and materials:** recorded by the people who use them, job by job.
- **Tracking:** see the status of every job in one place.
