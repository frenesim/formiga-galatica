<p class="lead"><strong>Agile Booking</strong> is a short-term rental management tool we built for companies that manage holiday homes on behalf of their owners. It has been in use since 2015.</p>

## The problem

Bookings, cleaning and payments lived in emails, spreadsheets and the manager's head.

## What it does

- Bookings from Airbnb and other platforms arrive on their own, read from emails.
- Calendars stay in sync, so there are no double bookings.
- Guests fill in their details online and the mandatory report to AIMA (the Portuguese Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum) is prepared automatically.
- Each person sees only what they need: manager, cleaning team, reception, accountant and owner.
- Staff send a message or voice note on Telegram and AI turns it into a task, with a priority and a deadline based on the next guest arrival.
