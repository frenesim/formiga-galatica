<p class="lead">Accounting firms, law offices, clinics and estate agents have tasks that repeat every week: asking for documents, booking appointments, reminding people of dates, filling in client records, producing reports. We build simple, made-to-measure tools that take that repeated work off people's hands.</p>

## Problems we solve

- Asking clients for documents by email, WhatsApp and phone, then filing files with different names.
- Appointments and reminders done by hand.
- Each client's details scattered across spreadsheets, emails and paper.
- Reports made by hand, copying numbers from one place to another.
- The same information typed two or three times in different places.

## What a tool can do

- **Document collection:** the client gets the list of what is missing, uploads the files in one place, and the office sees on one page who has delivered and who hasn't.
- **Appointments and reminders:** notices sent on the agreed day, without anyone having to remember.
- **Client records in one place:** details are typed once and used everywhere.
- **Reports:** built from the data that already exists, instead of copied by hand.
