What changed

Until recently, custom software was for big companies: tens of thousands of euros and months of work.

Today, with new AI tools, an experienced developer builds in days or weeks what used to take months. That means a small business can now have a tool designed around how it actually works — for a fraction of the price.

But we know something: most people don't care about computers. They want the problem gone, not another system to look after.

So we do all the work for you. You explain the problem; we handle the rest.
