Short-term rentals · In use since 2015

Agile Booking — holiday rental management

For companies that manage holiday homes on behalf of their owners. Bookings, cleaning and payments used to live in emails, spreadsheets and the manager's head.

  • Airbnb and other platform bookings come in by themselves, read from the emails.
  • Calendars stay in sync, so nothing gets double-booked.
  • Guests fill in their details online, and the mandatory guest report to AIMA, the Portuguese immigration agency, is prepared automatically.
  • Everyone sees only what they need: manager, cleaning team, check-in staff, accountant and owner.
  • Staff send a message or voice note on Telegram and AI turns it into a task, with a priority and a deadline based on the next guest arrival.

Industrial services · In use since 2020

Tekclean — custom sales management

Tekclean works with industrial cleaning equipment (laser, ultrasound, aqueous washing). Its sales team tracked clients, visits and proposals in spreadsheets; now everything is in one place.

This tool runs the company's daily agenda — and is the foundation for taking its services nationwide.

  • Type in a tax number (NIF) and the company's details and business sector are filled in automatically.
  • Every sales opportunity has a clear status, from first contact to signed contract.
  • Calls, visits and meetings show up straight in the phone's calendar.
  • Proposals and contracts with each client's services and machines, plus a record of competitors' contracts.
